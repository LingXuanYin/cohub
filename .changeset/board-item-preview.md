Extension items take a `preview` (`title`, `excerpt`, `cover`, `src`). Clients and exports without the type's renderer draw it as a file card, and item titles, including the selection sent to agents, use it. `boards schema extension` describes it.

Hosts: write `preview` on your own item types and keep it current.
